Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Cancelling a subscription

  • Cancel any time in Account → Manage billing in the app or at companion.gapapp.app, or email gap.app.team@gmail.com.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds

Free trial
The 7-day trial is free and asks for no card, so nothing is charged when it ends.
Any charge
Ask within 7 days of a charge, the first or a renewal, and we refund it in full if you have not used GAP Companion since it, for example if you forgot to cancel.
Our mistakes
If we charge you by mistake, for example after you cancelled, we refund it in full whenever you tell us.
Otherwise
We do not refund the unused part of a period that was correctly charged, except where the law requires it.

Your legal rights

Consumer law in your country, such as the EU and UK right to withdraw from a digital purchase or other statutory guarantees, is not limited by this policy.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show them. A refund ends the plan it covers, and your access stops at that point.

Disputes

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. We resolve most billing problems the same day.
