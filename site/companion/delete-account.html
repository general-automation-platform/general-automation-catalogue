Delete your account

For GAP Companion (app.gapapp.companion) users. Last updated October 17, 2026.

How to request deletion

  1. Email gap.app.team@gmail.com with the subject Delete my GAP Companion account.
  2. Send it from the email address of the account you sign in with, so we can confirm it is you.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (sign-in ids and email address) and sign-in sessions.
  • Linked devices, their reported status, events, totals and command history.
  • Your workflows, presets and notification settings, including push tokens.
  • Any screenshots still stored (they are removed automatically after 7 days anyway).

What is kept

  • Payment records held by Stripe, which Stripe must keep under financial and tax law. We keep only the invoice references the same laws require.
  • Short-lived server logs, which expire within a few days.

Before you delete

Cancel an active subscription first in Account → Manage billing, otherwise it can renew until we process your request. Deleting does not uninstall GAP from your devices, and removing the app does not delete your data.

Want only to stop a device reporting? Unlink it in the app. That keeps your account and removes its live data.
