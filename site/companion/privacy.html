Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Early access

GAP Companion is a phone app (app.gapapp.companion), a web dashboard (companion.gapapp.app) and a server (cloud.gapapp.app) provided by Thi Nguyen, doing business as General Automation Platform ("we"). It lets you watch and control the GAP automation app on Android devices you link to your account. This page explains what we store, why, and how to have it deleted. Questions: gap.app.team@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account
When you sign in: the account id and email address from Google, Apple or Discord, or the address you type to get an email code. Nothing else from those profiles. Sign-in codes and links are kept only as hashes and are deleted once used or soon after they expire. We also note the country you signed up from, taken from your network connection, to store your data in a nearby region and to refuse regions we cannot serve.
Devices you link
Each device's name, model, Android version and GAP version, and when it connected and disconnected.
What your devices report
The status of the script running on each device, its recent events, running totals (for example runs and results), the list of scripts it has, and the commands you send it with their results.
What you save
The workflows and presets you create in the app.
Screenshots you request
If you ask a device for a screenshot, the picture is stored briefly so you can view it, and is deleted after 7 days.
Notifications
Your phone's push notification token and which kinds of notification you turned on.
Payments
Stripe handles every payment. We keep only the Stripe customer and subscription references, your plan and when it ends. We never see or store your card details.
Technical logs
Our host keeps request logs, including IP addresses, for a few days to keep the Service secure and working.

What we do not collect

  • No contacts, photos, files or precise location from your phone.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or rent your data.

Google user data

We use Google Sign-In only to identify you. We receive your Google account id and email address, and use them to create and secure your account, to contact you about it, and to match your purchases to it. We request no other Google data and no access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts or Calendar.

GAP Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not let people read it except to provide support you asked for, to follow the law, or to keep the Service secure.

How we use it

Only to run GAP Companion: signing you in, showing and controlling your devices, syncing your workflows and presets, sending the notifications you asked for, knowing which plan you have, keeping the Service secure, and answering your messages. We may email you about your account, billing or the alpha.

Cookies and this website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. The web dashboard sets one first-party cookie to keep you signed in, and stores your theme choice in your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare runs our servers and stores the data above.
  • Google, Apple and Discord provide sign-in (you choose one) and tell us your account id and email address. Google provides push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Apple delivers them on iOS.
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails, so it sees your email address and the message.
  • Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy.

These providers act on our instructions or under their own terms. Your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards these providers offer.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Recent events are trimmed automatically to the newest ones, and screenshots are deleted after 7 days. A device you unlink stays in your account's command history, without its live data. After deletion, backups expire within 30 days.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to stop processing it, wherever you live (including under the GDPR and the CCPA). To delete your account and data, follow these steps. For other requests email gap.app.team@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Signing out of the app does not delete anything; unlinking a device stops it reporting.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, and access to production is limited to the people who run the Service. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us if you suspect a problem.

Children

GAP Companion is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date, and tell alpha testers by email when the change matters.
