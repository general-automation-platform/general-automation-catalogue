Common questions

How do I link a device?

Sign in to GAP Companion (with Google or Discord), open Link a device, then scan the code shown in GAP's cloud settings on the device (or type the code in).

My device shows offline

Check that the device has network access and that GAP is running with its cloud link turned on. It reconnects on its own once it can reach the internet.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

Open Account in the app or at companion.gapapp.app and choose Manage billing. See also the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
