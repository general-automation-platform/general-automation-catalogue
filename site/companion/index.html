What it does

Your whole lab, from a couch

GAP runs your JavaScript automation on each phone, tablet or emulator. GAP Companion is the paid layer on top: a secure cloud service plus an app and dashboard that connect to every GAP device you own.

  • Live device status

    Which devices are online, what each script is doing right now, and how long it has been running.

  • Results and history

    Totals, per-run results and a recent-events timeline for each device, so you can see what changed and when.

  • Alerts that matter

    Push notifications when a run finishes, fails or needs a decision. Choose which kinds you want.

  • Remote controls

    Start, pause, resume or stop a run, or stop after the current round, on one device or many at once.

  • Settings and presets

    Edit a script's settings from your phone, save them as presets and apply them to many devices at once.

  • Screenshots on request

    Ask a device for what its screen shows right now. Screenshots are deleted after seven days.

How it works

Three steps, no servers of your own

  • Run scripts with GAP

    Install GAP, the free Android automation app, on your devices: the starter tool puts it on each one. Run your scripts as usual.

  • Link your devices

    Sign in to GAP Companion. In GAP, open Settings → GAP Companion → Link to GAP Companion and scan the code it shows. Once per device.

  • Watch and steer

    Open the Android app or the web dashboard to see every device, review results, get alerts and send commands.

Interactive direction

When a script gets stuck, fix it from your phone

The phone app, in six real frames. Hover to pause.

A surprise permission dialog, a promo banner, a screen nobody planned for. Instead of failing the whole run, a script that supports it can pause and ask you what to do.

  1. The script pauses and you get an alertOnly on screens it does not recognise, so a run is not lost to one surprise.
  2. You see what the device seesA fresh screenshot opens in the app. Mark one tap or one drag where it should go.
  3. Press play, check, repeatThe device makes your tap itself and sends a new screenshot, so you can confirm it worked or try again.
  4. Continue hands control backThe script resumes from where it paused. If nobody answers within ten minutes, it falls back to its normal handling.

Questions

Do I need GAP too?

Yes. GAP is the free Android app that runs your scripts. GAP Companion connects to it and does not automate anything on its own. Set up GAP.

What can see my data?

Your devices report status, events and totals to your own account. We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics trackers. Read the Privacy Policy.

Which platforms are supported?

An Android app and a web dashboard that works in current browsers on desktop and phone. The iOS app is in development.

Which scripts get Companion features?

Every linked device shows its status, alerts and controls. Features inside a script, such as asking you for directions, reach signed scripts only, and signing for your own scripts is still to come.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel in the app or the billing portal and you keep access until the period you paid for ends. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
